## I: An Introduction to Cadriharetha

*(Repeated verbatim in every decade)*

**Note:** This is a series of opening chapters of stories that may be developed further elsewhere, each set on different lands on Cadriharetha, the Realmworld, ordered in sets of ten or "Livian Decades". The individual chapter titles state which continent or other location wherein the story takes place, followed by a date in A.H., the Human Years reckoning.

**Welcome to Cadriharetha!**

Cadriharetha is the name in the Mother Tongue for the planet the Realm sits on — site of most events in the Saga of the Coin, the Sword and the Medallion.

The star system is complex: primary **Cama**; Cycle of the Skies ~600 Earth years; Twins **Cadrima** (Desert) and **Nograca** (Ocean) in nested double orbits; moon **Iareca**; Trojans **Camacapa** and **Stediaca**; portent-star **Stapatre**.

Cadriharetha has ~5× Earth's surface area (~1 billion sq mi). As of A.H. 4000: 92 (formerly 93) continents ~Australia-sized. Notable exceptions: **Norodia** (New World / North-America-like), drowned **Magnadia**, **Ranadia** (Hollow Continent). Major cluster: **Pentaland Formation** around the Great Hub.

## III: Breed Descriptions (by source world)

**Breeds** = sapient groups that reproduce within-group without outside assistance (preferred over "races"/"species").

Common: Near-Humans / MBHs; Quaddies; Hexies; Bunnies; Slinkers; Fuzzies (H. Beam Piper homage); Centaurs (many varieties); Satyroids; Giants (High/Tall); Dragons; Armatures (AI mechanisms).

**Orphans** fall in from the Other Place. **Grand Compact of Aliens / Alien Combine** founding breeds: Triphibs, Dreefuzzes, Wheelies, Blaphirs, Glyvi, Swiphins, Araknants, Dagyrints.

Other Place also: Wellesians; Qaurachs/Scorpiacs; Xrglonthi; Bions.
