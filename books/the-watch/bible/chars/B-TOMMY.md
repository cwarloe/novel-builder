## Who he is when nobody is writing a theme
Tommy survives rooms by going still. He is not noble about it. Stillness is cheaper than being the event. He would rather lose a minute at the locker than give them a swing to film in their heads. He is loyal once someone sits with him, and that loyalty makes him easy to unbalance with a gift he cannot match.

He is not a nerd mascot and not a secret athlete. He reads science fiction he has already finished because a known ending cannot ambush him. He builds Legos after bad days because bricks go where he puts them. The laptop is a door he can shut.
