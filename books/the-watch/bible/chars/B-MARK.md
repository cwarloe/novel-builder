# B-MARK — full working bible

## Who he is when the theme is off
Mark is good at rooms until they get specific. He can find a shop, make a joke, put an object in someone’s hand. He is less good at staying standing when the room knows his street. He likes being the person who can do a thing. That is not the same as being safe.

He used to belong to basketball the way elementary kids belong to a team. Then he did not. Comics are the leftover that still works.

He is Black in a school that will use that when an expensive object appears. He is not poor. The rumor does not care.

## Locked

## Body and tells [OPTION]
Easy posture until the street is named, then the joke gets faster or the phone becomes a wall. Hands: case glass, a bill, Tommy’s shoulder once and not again if Tommy flinches.
Does not code-switch for the reader’s education.

## Voice
Light, then clipped. Nah man can mean get off him. He does not speech the rumor. If angry he will call it stupid before he calls it racist — not because it is not, because he is thirteen and wants the room to move.

## Can / cannot
Can: old town; produce the watch by some path; ice cream; shopkeeper.
Cannot: make the gift small; stay up when they name his house; keep 1993 from looking twice.

## Interior [OPTION]
Tommy is the first person who did not want the phone or the clothes. A gift can do what a joke cannot. He underestimates how public an object becomes on someone else’s wrist.

## Wound / lie / want / need [OPTION]

Need: a year that does not care about his phone.
