# STEP 3 — one-pagers (locked spine only)

## B-TOMMY
- One-line: A middle-schooler who treats silence as armor and cannot get his live-in father to hear the hallway.
- Motivation (abstract): to stop being the problem in every room.
- Goal (concrete): get through Lincoln without becoming the joke; keep Mark without owing him a life.
- Conflict: the watch gift makes him more visible; 1993 shows him David as a kid he cannot officially save; he still has to go to school on Monday.
- Epiphany [OPTION]: Dad’s useless advice was a badly aimed version of the same fear, not proof Dad never lived it.
- Not his job: heal David’s childhood; redeem Todd; speech the past forward.

## B-MARK
- One-line: A Black kid with medical-pro money whose friendship gestures are larger than Tommy can repay and larger than the hallway will read cleanly.
- Motivation: to keep the one friend who is not the old basketball crowd.
- Goal: get Tommy the watch; go to the shop; not be left on the cafeteria edge.
- Conflict: generosity looks like romance, theft, or charity depending on who is looking; 1993 will look at him twice.
- Epiphany [OPTION]: the gift was never going to buy safety; it only bought a witness.
- Open: pay or steal. Not open: romance, poverty-as-race.

## B-DAVID
- One-line: A father in the house who offers chess club because that is the tool he has.
- Motivation: not to fail his kid the way rooms failed him.
- Goal: get Tommy to talk / try / join something.
- Conflict: the tool is from 1993; the hallway is pre-smartphone; he cannot see young Davey in the boy at the door until after the jump exists as a shared fact.
- Epiphany [OPTION]: “I was you” is only useful if he stops using it as a closer.
- 1993 self (Davey): does not know the visitors; chess already a hole; no monologue.

## B-JEN
- One-line: The parent who converts worry into a phone call.
- Motivation: keep the house from splitting on a silence.
- Goal: find an adult at school who has seen what she has not.
- Conflict: the watch on the wrist; the “I’m fine”; she does not jump.
- Epiphany: optional and small. She is not the thesis.
