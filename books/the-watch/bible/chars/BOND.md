# BOND — Tommy and Mark

They bond by going the same direction long enough that turning back would be weirder than continuing.
Side-by-side, not knee-to-knee. Talk is about the road or the glass. Silence is fine.
Care: wait at the rack, save a seat, you coming or not, pay without ceremony.
Not: I got you, only friend, I know what you're going through.

S05 miles exist so the watch is not charity. Sweat before cash.
Tease on the ride is available. Pack jokes and the cafeteria fold are not bits.
2006: no texting the friendship into being. Flip phone is a wall, not a thread.

NOT FOR: loneliness confession, montage, brother I never had, thank-you speech, Mark coaching bravery.
Test: if an adult would be proud of the sentence, cut it. If it could be said at a chainring, keep it.
