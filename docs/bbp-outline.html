Audience: authors who use LLMs for fiction and want to keep control of canon

TITLE
The Author Locks the Canon. The LLM Follows Policy.
A human-locked canon kit for LLM-assisted fiction. It is not a novel generator.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
You're writing fiction with an LLM, and the story's facts must stay yours. You're the author who decides what is true in the story.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
LLMs fill gaps with plausible detail, and plausible detail quietly becomes canon. Copy the templates, lock the canon, and work the Snowflake steps in order. A story you control, with no invented canon.
Transition 1The kit puts the author in charge. Three ideas explain it: the policy, the build order, and the portable kit.
